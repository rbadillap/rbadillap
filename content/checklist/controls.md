title: Controls

order: 2
items:
  - Every control has an owner, leaves evidence, and runs on a schedule. Every process names who does it and who approves it.
  - Controls live inside the daily work of the team that does it. Nothing waits for a review after the fact.
  - Builders own quality, shippers own the automated gates, product owns purpose and acceptance, governance owns policy and exceptions.
  - Use one template per artifact, so a model card looks the same for every model.
  - Choose the level of rigor yourself. More gates cost speed, fewer cost control.
