title: Agents
slug: agents
order: 7
items:
  - Test generative models against the real use case for hallucination, bias, leaks of private data or IP, harmful output, and adversarial prompts.
  - Limit what an agent can do. Its tools, permissions, memory, and actions outside the system.
  - Keep a human able to review, set limits, escalate, and stop the system.
  - Log the model, prompt, sources, version, and every action taken.
  - Watch for degradation, abuse, and silent changes in third-party models.
  - Decide which uses are authorized and how much autonomy you accept. Revisit as the technology and the rules move.
