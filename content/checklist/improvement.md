---
title: Improvement
slug: improvement
order: 6
items:
  - Find where governance is slow, duplicated, error-prone, or manual.
  - Measure it. Time to approval, compliance rate, exceptions, manual effort.
  - Automate the controls that can be automated. Validation, approvals, evidence capture, alerts.
  - Improve from what happened. Audits, incidents, metrics, feedback.
  - Decide what stays under human eyes and how much you are willing to invest.
