---
title: Evidence
slug: evidence
order: 4
items:
  - Keep an inventory of every model. Owner, version, status, criticality, dependencies, where it runs.
  - Every model has a card. Purpose, data, method, limits, performance, known risks.
  - Every approval says who, what, when, under which conditions, and until when.
  - Evidence is tied to a model version and cannot be edited after the fact.
  - Decide who maintains the records, how often they are reviewed, and how long they are kept.
---
