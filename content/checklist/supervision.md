---
title: Supervision
slug: supervision
order: 5
items:
  - Check regularly that controls run and leave the evidence they promise.
  - Look for controls that are missing, failed, expired, or undocumented.
  - Rank each gap by impact and urgency. Report status, exceptions, and the risk that remains.
  - Every gap gets an owner and a date. It closes only when the fix is verified and the evidence kept.
  - Risk acceptance, priorities, and exceptions are signed by someone authorized to sign them.
