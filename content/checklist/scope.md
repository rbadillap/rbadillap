---
title: Scope
slug: scope
order: 1
items:
  - Know which models, datasets, providers, and environments you govern, and which you leave out.
  - Know which rules apply to you and where each one comes from.
  - Write down what governance will deliver and how you will know it worked.
  - Keep the big decisions yours. Policy, risk appetite, owners, tools. Advisors and agents flag them, they do not make them.
