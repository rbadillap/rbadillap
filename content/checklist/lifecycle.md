---
title: Lifecycle
slug: lifecycle
order: 3
items:
  - Register every model at intake with its purpose, owner, data, and risk.
  - Have someone who did not build the model validate it for performance, bias, and compliance.
  - Do not deploy without monitoring and a way to roll back.
  - Set the thresholds for drift and incidents before release, not after the first one.
  - Treat every change as a small release. Assess, approve, record.
  - Retire models on purpose. Switch them off, keep the evidence, tell whoever depends on them.
---
